Not every buyer is local, and we don't think that should limit which car you
end up with. Here's how delivery actually works.

**Enclosed transport, always.** Exotic and collector cars ship enclosed, not
on an open rack next to daily drivers. It costs more. We don't cut that
corner.

**Photos and video at handoff.** Before the car leaves our shop, you get a
full walk-around on video — matched again at delivery, so there's a clear
record of condition on both ends.

**Real delivery windows.** We give you a transport company, a tracking
contact, and an honest timeline — not a vague "a few weeks" and radio
silence.

**Paperwork handled.** Title, registration support, and temp tags where
applicable, sorted before the truck shows up so the car is actually ready to
drive the day it arrives.

Nationwide delivery is available on every car in our collection — ask about
it when you request more information on a listing.

Every car we list goes through the same process before it's offered for sale —
no exceptions, regardless of how clean it looks on arrival.

**Mechanical.** A full inspection covering the drivetrain, suspension,
brakes, and any known problem areas for that specific model. If a car needs
work to be sale-ready, it gets the work before it gets a listing.

**Cosmetic.** Paint depth readings on every panel to catch prior bodywork that
doesn't show up in photos, plus a full detail so you're seeing the car as it
actually is, not as a wash can make it look.

**Paperwork.** Title check, service records, and a search for any accident or
salvage history. If something doesn't check out, the car doesn't get listed —
simple as that.

**Test drive.** Our own inspector drives every car before it goes live, not
just the seller's word on how it runs.

It's slower than just posting photos and a price. It's also why buyers come
back to us instead of rolling the dice on a private sale.

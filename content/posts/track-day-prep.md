Buying the car is the easy part. Showing up to your first track day prepared
is where most owners get caught out.

A few things we walk every buyer through before their first session:

- **Tire pressures, cold and hot.** Street pressures are wrong for track work —
  know your target hot pressures before you're already two sessions in.
- **Brake fluid age.** Track temperatures boil street-spec fluid fast. If it's
  been more than a year, flush it first.
- **Fluid levels, not just oil.** Coolant and brake fluid take a beating under
  sustained load — check them cold, the morning of.
- **A pre-track inspection.** Lug torque, brake pad wear, fluid leaks — the
  same 20-minute checklist our shop runs before delivery.

None of this is complicated, but skipping it is how a great car day turns into
a tow-truck call. Bring your car by before your next event and we'll run
through it with you at no charge.
